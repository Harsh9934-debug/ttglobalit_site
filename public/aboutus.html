Our Story

Built by domain experts, powered by technology

Transcript Technology Global Infotech Private Limited is a technology company catering to financial and educational institutions. The company is founded by people with proven experience and an in-depth understanding of financial and education enterprises, combined with deep technology expertise.

We developed the Finance Management systems E-Sahakara and E-Vyavahaar for modern banking and GST billing using cutting-edge technology. Our solutions help institutions save money, save time, and make the best of their resources.

From concept to shining reality, we provide everything our clients need to bring their brand to life in the digital world.

Our Journey

A decade of transformation

Key milestones that shaped TT GLOBAL IT into the trusted technology partner it is today.

The Beginning

Founded with a vision to simplify financial technology for cooperative banks and small institutions.

E-Sahakara Launch

Introduced our flagship core banking solution, adopted by cooperative societies across Karnataka.

Product Diversification

Scaling Nationwide
