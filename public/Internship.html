FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about applying, eligibility, and program execution.

Who is eligible to apply for this internship?

Students pursuing B.E., B.Tech, BCA, MCA, B.Sc (Computer Science / IT), or Diploma in Engineering, as well as recent graduates looking to transition into modern software development, are welcome to apply. Prior project experience or open-source curiosity is an added plus.

What are the key benefits and takeaways?

During your internship with TT GLOBAL IT, you will:

  • Work directly on enterprise software architectures with production deployments
  • Receive dedicated 1-on-1 code reviews and mentorship from senior engineers
  • Earn a verified Certificate of Completion and personalized Letter of Recommendation
  • Qualify for direct Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) upon graduation
  • Complete academic final-year project requirements with formal documentation
Will I receive an official certificate and college NOC?

Yes! Upon successful completion of your internship project, you receive an official verified Certificate of Completion, a formal project synopsis report for your university viva, and an offer letter / NOC prior to commencement if requested by your college department.

Can single students apply or is there a minimum batch size?

There is no minimum batch size. Individual students can apply at any time. College project groups of 2 to 4 students are also welcome to apply together to build integrated capstone projects.

What are the work locations and modes?

Internships are available at our offices in Bengaluru (Hebbal) and Mysuru (KIADB), as well as in hybrid and remote formats for students completing college semesters across Karnataka and nationwide.
