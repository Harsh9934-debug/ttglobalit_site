About ECHITS

Chit Fund Management Reimagined

TTGLOBALIT ECHITS is a comprehensive digital platform purpose-built for managing chit fund operations. It covers every aspect of the chit cycle — from member registration and auction management to dividend calculations, payment tracking, and regulatory compliance.

ECHITS eliminates manual processes and paperwork, making chit fund management transparent, efficient, and compliant with all applicable regulations. Whether you run a single group or hundreds, ECHITS scales with your business.

Key Features

Complete Chit Fund Features

Everything you need to run a compliant chit fund business

Member Management

Onboard and manage members with KYC verification, nominee tracking, and complete member lifecycle management.

Auction Management

Conduct transparent auctions with bid management, winner selection, and automated foreman commission calculations.

Dividend Calculation

Automatic dividend computation for each installment based on auction discounts with precise per-member statements.

Payment Tracking

Real-time installment collection tracking with automated reminders, receipts, and defaulter management.

Compliance & Reports

Generate statutory reports for regulatory submissions including RCS filings and auditor-ready financial statements.

Multi-group Support

Manage unlimited chit groups simultaneously with individual group dashboards and consolidated company-level reporting.
