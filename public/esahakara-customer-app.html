Customer Application

Banking in Your Pocket

The Esahakara Customer Mobile App puts the power of banking directly in your customers' hands. With an intuitive interface and robust security, users can manage their savings accounts, view transaction history, track recurring deposits, and access banking services anytime, anywhere.

Designed for a seamless mobile experience, the app brings the full capabilities of the Esahakara core banking system to customers, enabling self-service, real-time updates, and enhanced financial awareness.
