FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an internship?

An internship is an on-site work experience directly related to your major or career interest. It can be paid or unpaid, held during summer or throughout the academic year.

What are the benefits of the internship?

You get the opportunity to:

  • Gain valuable work experience before graduation
  • Develop new skills and refine existing ones
  • Apply coursework knowledge to real on-the-job situations
  • Reality-test career choices before committing
  • Build a professional network and get references
Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every intern receives a Certificate of Completion upon finishing the program.

What documents do I need to bring?

If you need a letter before arriving, email ttglobalinfotech7@gmail.com with all student names, registration numbers, college name, and addressee for the letter.

Is there a minimum group size to apply?

No minimum. Even a single student can participate — there is no maximum limit either.
